# SIM-517 — the catcher RECEIVING profile: the build plan (2026-09-03)

**The owner design (2026-08-29, absorbing SIM-484; REVISED by the owner
2026-09-03):** ONE catcher receiving profile — one embedding, one similarity
score. The receiving weight conditions the PITCH draw; **the dropped third
strike is NOT a separate sampling step** — the drawn pitch row itself carries
whether the ball got away (passed ball / wild pitch), and that flag resolves
the play: on a strike-3 the batter takes first (when the rule allows); with
runners on they advance. The profile carries passed-ball and wild-pitch
occurrences generally, not just dropped-third-strike occurrences. Everything
follows the standing architecture rule: the factor is a draw WEIGHT (or off),
the drawn row IS the play, and the fitted kernel replaces the legacy flip on
certification.

**Sequencing (owner ruling 2026-09-03):** SIM-517 first; then SIM-520
certifies R on a broader, defense-diverse game sample.

## What exists today (the starting state)

* `_apply_framing` (sim_loop.py) is a POST-DRAW flip on taken pitches —
  default OFF since the 2026-08-29 ruling; `SIM_FRAMING=1` is an A/B opt-in
  only. It reads `catcher_framing()` (the centred called-strike delta) from
  the catcher embedding, which already carries `strikes_above_average` /
  `pitches_received_total`.
* The catcher computor already computes framing, blocking and throwing
  metrics (`_compute_catcher_framing/_blocking/_throwing` →
  `derived.catcher_season_metrics`).
* The PITCH pool (`HandPool`) carries NO per-row catcher_id — the pitch-draw
  weight needs a pool rebuild (the steal pool already carries catcher_id; the
  batted-ball pool gained p_throws/venue_id/bat_home the same way).
* The dropped third strike is a resolver stub that NEVER fires in production
  (`_dropped_third_strike`: eligible on a swinging strike-3 with 1B open or
  two outs, but no signal is injected) — the channel is missing entirely, and
  the reach is committed with event "field_error", which needs reconciling
  with the pool's own label.
* `raw.play_events` is filled (the SIM-488 re-sweep), so per-pitch event
  detail exists to label uncaught third strikes.

## Part A — the receiving profile (data + embedding)

Extend `derived.catcher_season_metrics` (and the catcher actor embedding)
with the receiving block, all measured per opportunity from the re-swept
data:

1. **Framing** — already present: called strikes above expected per taken
   pitch (`strikes_above_average`, `pitches_received_total`, the shadow/heart
   zone columns from SIM-408).
2. **Ball-got-away rates (owner revision 2026-09-03)** — passed-ball rate
   AND wild-pitch-allowed rate per receiving opportunity, plus the uncaught
   strike-3 rate per eligible strike-3. All three are the same physical
   skill read at three denominators, and all three enter the ONE receiving
   score. The blocking computor exists; verify its columns survive into the
   embedding, and add the strike-3 read. Label the get-away pitches in
   `raw.pitches`/`raw.play_events` (wild_pitch / passed_ball on the pitch
   where they occur; a strikeout where the batter reached first = uncaught
   strike-3). Measure the league rates first — they anchor the pool bands
   and sanity-check the labeling (hundreds of games, per the standing ETL
   validation rule).

One RECEIVING similarity score: an RBF over the standardized receiving
features (the fielder-kernel pattern; the throwing/steal features stay in the
steal ensemble — receiving is its own score).

DuckDB migration + `POOL_BUILDER_VERSION` bump + the catcher-metrics
recompute (catcher computor only — not the full profile chain).

## Part C — consumer 1: the taken-pitch draw weight

Weight pitch-pool rows by the RECEIVING similarity between the live catcher
and the row's own catcher, env-gated `SIM_CATCHER_KERNEL_SIGMA` (0 = off,
byte-identical — the CDF-equality test pattern).

**The SIM-476 normalization lesson applies from day one:** the factor must
shift only what it is entitled to shift — which pitch OUTCOMES get drawn,
given the live catcher — never the count-bucket structure or the pitch-count
distribution. Normalize the factor to a mean of 1 within each count bucket
(the per-position precedent, property-tested before any arm runs), and make
missing-identity rows exactly neutral (the mean-valid-weight lesson).

On certification, DELETE `_apply_framing`, `catcher_framing()` and the
`SIM_FRAMING` env (the standing flip-deletion ritual).

## Part D — consumer 2: resolving the drawn row's `got_away` flag

No second draw (owner revision 2026-09-03). When the drawn pitch row carries
`got_away`:

* **Strike-3** (the drawn outcome ends the PA as a strikeout): the batter
  takes first WHEN the official rule allows (1B open, or two outs); forced
  runners push exactly like a walk. When the rule does not allow the reach,
  the runners still advance — the ball still got away. This retires the
  never-fires `_dropped_third_strike` resolver stub, and the event commits
  with the label the pool itself uses for an uncaught K3 (a strikeout +
  reach, not a bare "field_error").
* **Any other pitch with runners on:** the runners advance one base (a
  wild pitch / passed ball). This channel is MISSING from the simulator
  today — landing it adds real runner traffic, so the certifying lane read
  comes with a league-volume check (MLB WP+PB ≈ 0.35/team-game).

The receiving weight is what makes the frequency conditional: a good
receiver behind the plate draws fewer get-away rows, a bad one more, at the
pool's own conditional rates.

**PARTS C + D BUILT (2026-09-03).** Part C: `FullPoolSampler.catcher_sigma`
(env `SIM_CATCHER_KERNEL_SIGMA`, 0 = byte-identical off) weights the pitch
draw by an RBF over four derived receiving features — the two zone-framing
rates, the block rate (`actual_pbwp / pitches_received_total`, derived at
build time), and the EB uncaught-K3 rate — z-scored over the catcher
embedding; the factor is normalized to a MEAN of 1 within each COUNT BUCKET
(the SIM-476 per-partition lesson, property-tested BEFORE any arm runs), and
missing-identity rows are exactly neutral. The live catcher joins the
half-inning key in `_full_pool_outcome`. Part D: `SIM_GOT_AWAY` (default
OFF) honors the drawn row: `draw()` remembers its row,
`last_pitch_got_away()` reads the flag, a non-terminal got-away advances
every runner one base (scoring from third, no RBI — the run routed through
`_commit_run_delta`, the steal-of-home accounting mirror; skipped when a
steal/pickoff already resolved the pitch), `_dropped_third_strike` now fires
on the drawn fact (the injected-resolver hook stays as the test path), and a
got-away strike-3 that CANNOT award first still advances the runners.
16 new consumer tests + the full regression gate green. Remaining: part E
(fit `catcher_sigma`, enable `SIM_GOT_AWAY=1` + the sigma in production +
lane flags, the two new pool bands, DELETE the `_apply_framing` flip).

## Part E — fit → enable → certify

1. **Probe** (`scripts/sim517_catcher_probe.py`, the fielder-probe pattern:
   wrap-once, control-subtracted, per-tier): sim called-strike share on taken
   pitches AND sim got-away rate per pitch received, each conditional on the
   live catcher's receiving tier vs the pool's own per-tier rates. Receiving
   tiers = recency-weighted terciles of the pool rows' catchers.
2. **Fit**: the largest sigma whose per-tier reads sit inside the band
   floors; seed-split confirmation (`--seed-base`, the park precedent).
3. **Certify**: the 12×500 lane, with two NEW pool bands —
   `CALLED_STRIKE_TAKEN` (called strikes per taken pitch) and `GOT_AWAY_PITCH`
   (passed-ball/wild-pitch rows drawn per pitch received; uncaught strike-3s
   are its strike-3 slice) — centres = the pool's own recency rates. Land the
   fitted sigma in docker-compose + the lane's `PRODUCTION_FLAGS` in the same
   commit; delete the framing flip with it.

**PART E FIT LADDER (in progress, 2026-09-04; 12×200 per arm, SIM_GOT_AWAY=1).**
The control shows the clean kernel-off defect: sim rates flat across live
tiers while the pool conditionals spread (framing high-tier −0.87pp; blocking
2× between tiers), and the got-away volume lands on MLB (0.343/team-game).
The framing channel closes monotonically with sigma (high-tier delta
−0.87 → −0.58 → −0.32 → −0.13pp at σ off/0.5/0.25/0.15) but blocking barely
responds (high-tier −0.080 → −0.068 at 0.15) and the got-away marginal
drifts low (0.343→0.307/tg). Diagnosis, measured: identity coverage is
99.96% (NOT missing data), the game set's catchers are composition-neutral
(their own-row expectation 0.00228 ≈ the 0.002325 marginal), and the live
catchers cluster tightly around league average — the under-delivery is
soft-kernel SHRINKAGE toward the dense centre of the catcher population (the
same algebra as the home kernel's (1−w)/(1+w) dilution). σ=0.08 and σ=0.05
arms are running to find where blocking converges; the fit stays the largest
sigma that closes both channels.
